STELLENPILOT FOR MAC: FIRST START

1. Drag Stellenpilot onto the Applications folder in this window.

2. The app is not signed by Apple yet, so macOS stops it the first time.

   macOS 15 (Sequoia) and newer:
   - Double click Stellenpilot in Applications. Click "Done" in the message.
   - Open System Settings, then Privacy & Security.
   - Scroll down to Security, click "Open Anyway" next to Stellenpilot.
   - Enter your Mac password and click "Open Anyway" again.

   macOS 14 and older:
   - Hold Control, click Stellenpilot in Applications, choose Open, then Open.

3. From then on it opens with a double click.

Says "damaged"? Open Terminal and run:
   xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Stellenpilot.app

Your files go to the folder "Stellenpilot" in your home folder.
More help: https://github.com/srvskr1/stellenpilot/blob/main/docs/mac.md
