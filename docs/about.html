About

Stellenpilot is built by Sourav Sarker, a Data Science master's student who lives in Essen.

Stellenpilot finds new jobs in Germany every morning, scores them against your CV and drafts a tailored CV and cover letter for the best ones. It runs on your own computer, so your CV stays there. It is free and open source under the MIT license, and the code is on GitHub.

The blog and the German level checker use the same rules the app uses to read job ads. When a guide gives a language level or a rule of thumb, it says whether that is an official standard or a common reading.

How it is built

Stellenpilot is built with an AI pair programmer. The decisions are mine.
