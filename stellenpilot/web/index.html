      2.

      3.

    Email
    
          
    Stellenpilot can read your application emails and move jobs forward by itself: a confirmation to Applied, an invitation to Interview, a rejection to Rejected. It only reads. It never sends, deletes, moves or labels mail, and nothing is marked as read.
